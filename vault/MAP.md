# MAP
How the vault is laid out. See [[AGENTS]] for the rules.

| Folder | Owner | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| `raw/` | read-only | Original sources: `articles/`, `pdfs/`, `images/`, `data/` |
| `notes/` | human | Your own notes (Second Brain) |
| `evidence/` | human | Raw data, screenshots, receipts |
| `wiki/sources/` | AI | One summary per raw source |
| `wiki/entities/` | AI | People, companies, tools, places |
| `wiki/concepts/` | AI | Ideas, methods, frameworks |
| `wiki/comparisons/` | AI | Side-by-side analyses |
| `index.md` | AI | Catalogue of every wiki page |
| `log.md` | AI | Append-only record of ingests, queries, lints |
