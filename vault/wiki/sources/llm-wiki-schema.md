# LLM Wiki Schema
The rulebook for this vault: Obsidian is the IDE, the AI is the programmer and the vault is the codebase. (raw/articles/llm-wiki-schema.md)

## Key points
- Three layers: `raw/` (read-only), `notes/` + `evidence/` (human), `wiki/` (AI-owned). (raw/articles/llm-wiki-schema.md)
- Each page has one topic, a kebab-case filename and YAML frontmatter. (raw/articles/llm-wiki-schema.md)
- Three operations: Ingest, Query and Lint. (raw/articles/llm-wiki-schema.md)
