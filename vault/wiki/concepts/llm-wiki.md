# LLM Wiki
A knowledge base where an AI keeps summary, entity and concept pages up to date from a fixed set of sources, citing each claim. (raw/articles/llm-wiki-schema.md)

## Why it works
- Sources never change, so every claim can be traced back. (raw/articles/llm-wiki-schema.md)
- Lint passes keep pages consistent as sources grow. (raw/articles/llm-wiki-schema.md)
